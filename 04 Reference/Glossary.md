# Glossary
- **Object:** one migratable thing, e.g. a customer master.
- **Reconciliation:** proving what landed matches what left (counts and values).
- **Mock run:** a full rehearsal against real data (brief's definition). **Load cycle:** one such load; our data has MOCK1–3.
- **Cutover:** the weekend the business switches systems.
- **Entity:** a legal entity / business unit migrating as a wave (E1–E5 in our data).
- **Run ledger:** DynamoDB table holding every claim, evidence, verdict, attempt count and tokens per run.
- **Critic:** agent that judges a claim against its evidence without seeing the plan or retry count.
