# Graders are AI and read only what is written
Scoring is done by four AI reviewers against written criteria. The business-case reviewer is told: "if something isn't in the document, the team gets no credit for it", and it rewards named roles, real numbers and stated assumptions over polish.

**Why:** [[Pitch track grading rubric]].
**How to apply:** Put every claim, number and assumption in writing in the deck, business case, spec or repo, not just in what we say aloud. Specific beats pretty.
