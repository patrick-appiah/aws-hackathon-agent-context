# Five deliverables, not just a repo
Graded: **pitch deck (20)**, **business case (20)**, **product spec (10)**, **GitHub repo (50)**, plus an on-site demo to the judges. Nothing needs to be hosted or sent as a link.

**Why:** [[Pitch track grading rubric]].
**How to apply:** Give the deck, business case and spec owners from Day 1 (the business/docs pair). The spec needs completeness, feasibility, priorities, user stories and measurable success targets.
