# Rule of evidence caps claims at 2
Any claim not shown in the demo, repo or architecture diagram caps that rubric criterion at 2/4.

**Why:** Judging rubric.
**How to apply:** Every claim in the pitch needs a screen, a file or a diagram behind it.
